# The Traveler

Once upon a time, a stranger without a name wearing an old cloak and holding a staff, arrived in a small village far away from an unknown place, some people believed it was close to North Camelot; other rumours suggested it was on the border of Rubra.
Wherever the place was, the important thing to the traveler was that he had finally found Florarial. A myth surrounds this place: a small blue flower with a magical light inside its petals that can heal any sickness, the plant that gave the base of village’s name, Floral.
He had a purpose to pursue in this place…

A pair of twins, Klein e Fier, lived and grew up in Florarial both were responsible for picking the flowers in the garden, as the family’s livelihood depended on selling them.

Every morning, the twin went to pick the flowers, Klein would pick all the colourful and beautiful ones, however, his brother was different. Fier only picked flowers that could be used as medicine or were useful to daily life, and he never took more than enough.

On that day, both were coming back home with all their flowers when they ran into the traveler. He was whispering words the likes of which they had never heard before. Although Klein ignored him and continued on his path, Fier decided to listen to him.

Suddenly the stranger looked into him eyes, he talked to him while he picked a small and innocent flower:

”Some Flowers can be a plague in an important space, while others are the faith to the garden.”

After picking the flower with finesse, he gave it to Klein: 

“Some flowers need to be picked up, ripped from the root… To ensure the future of all gardens.”

The next day, Klein kept thinking about what the stranger had told him.

While he was harvesting, he found the legendary flower, the Floral. It was a unique among the many beautiful blossoms, the light within captured his attention, drawing his eyes toward it.

He decided not to pick it, instead to preserve it so it could spread and save many lives in the future.
At the end of his harvesting, he was satisfied and hopeful with the future, he couldn’t believe the myth was actually true. Nevertheless, on this day, the winds were against his glimpse future.

Klein was shocked to see his brother holding the Floral, admiring the beauty.

He was speechless, he wasn’t angry… but the same light of the flower revealed the truth. As they walked back home, while Klein was distracted by the Floral, his brother picked up a heavy stone and struck him in the head, ensuring the Klein’s death.

Feir’s hands were covered with blood, he wasn’t upset. He was disappointed.

When Fier looked around, he realized the Traveler had watched everything. The stranger ignored him before leaving, looking satisfied.

Now, the traveler could leave from this village, and depart for the next one.

Both twins harvested the flowers, but only one plucked with purpose.
