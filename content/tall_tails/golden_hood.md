# Golden Hood

The trail must be followed.

The trail was a long and dangerous, filled with many creatures and bandits. 

The trail was abundant with lies and blood.

A sweet little girl called Golden Hood who lived in a small cottage far away from any city. She walked the trail among the Galahad trees to sell the cakes she had baked.

She was brave and wasn’t intimidated by the eyes staring through the dark.

Despite all difficulties, her hood was blessed by a god. It protected her and lit the correct path.

All the devils and creatures in the woods are just waiting for the day she forgets her hood.

Every day, Golden Hood stops at the border of Galahad, and gives her cakes to the adventurers, merchants and wanderers on the path.

Every night, she goes back along the same trail. At night, she prays to her god while keep the hood guides her safely to her sweet home.

Eventually, she keeps doing it every day.

Occasionally, you keep reading it line after line.

A good story is a linear trail.
