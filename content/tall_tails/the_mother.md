# The Mother

Once upon a time, a stranger without a name wearing an old cloak and holding a staff, arrived in a small village far away from an unknown place, some people believed it was close to North Camelot; other rumours suggested it was on the border of Rubra.
Wherever the place was, the important thing to the traveler was that he had finally found Florarial. A myth surrounds this place: a small blue flower with a magical light inside its petals that can heal any sickness, the plant that gave the base of village’s name, Floral.
He had a purpose to pursue in this place…

A pair of twins, Klein e Fier, lived and grew up in Florarial both were responsible for picking the flowers in the garden, as the family’s livelihood depended on selling them.

Every morning, the twin went to pick the flowers, Klein would pick all the colourful and beautiful ones because they were the easiest to sell, however, his brother was different. Fier was slightly lazy and most of the time didn’t gather enough flowers. Every time, Klein needed to compensate for him.

On that day, both were coming back home with all their flowers when they ran into the traveler. He was whispering words the likes of which they had never heard before. Although Fier stopped to listen to the stranger and wasting their time, Klein decided to go straight to the market to sell the flowers, because their mother was waiting for help at home.

At home, their mother was sick, he knew it was a terminal illness. All nights he prayed to find out how to save her.

That night, Klein dreamed of the stranger in that he had ignored, in the same path, in the same day… however now he understood every word he had spoken it.

“Hope blossoms in the middle of chaos, but salvation only blossoms for the one who can pluck it.”

The next day, Klein kept thinking about what the stranger had told him.

While he was harvesting, he found the legendary flower, the Floral. It was a unique among the many beautiful blossoms, the light within captured his attention, drawing his eyes toward it.

He decided to pick it, believing it could finally save their mother’s life.

When he showed to his brother, Fier was shocked and speechless. As they walked back home, while Klein was distracted by the Floral, his brother picked up a heavy stone and struck him in the head, ensuring the Klein’s death.

Before Klein’s death, he could hear Fier whispering unknown words but for some reason he understood, Klein died smiling.

Feir’s hands were covered with blood, he wasn’t upset. He was indifferent.

Finally he took the Floral, and went to save his mother. Now, the traveler could leave from this village, and depart for the next one.

Both twins harvested the flowers, but only one plucked with hope.
