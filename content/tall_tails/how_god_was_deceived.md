# How God Was Deceived

When a baby is born, he is bald. There is not a single strand of hair on his head.
When an elderly person is close to death. There is not a single strand of hair on his head.

Each strand is a memory, an experience in our lives.

A pastor, who believed that Beta’s balance was corrupted, was watching all his flock being judged for their actions.

Matriel was an elf who lived a long life — so long that she saw nations destroyed and born from the ashes with her own eyes. In her experience, she realized a feeling that persisted through all stories, a piece of corruption in every soul.

Greed, a thought that grows like a parasite in their minds, devouring their good deeds and pushing all humanity into the abyss of oblivion.

Matriel cared about all lives. To her, the balance was important, good and bad could coexist together and be happy enough to not be judged by Beta.

One day, Matriel was visited by a golden pigeon, it was a Beta’s invitation to become a herald.

Instantly, she killed the pigeon and refused it.

The next day, Matriel was visited by a golden cow, it was another invitation from Beta. Rapidly, she killed it and refused again.

The day after that, Matriel was visited by a golden carp, she knew it was an invitation. However, she still kept refusing.

The God was perplexed that a simple mortal could refuse all his gifts, when It looked closer it to her, It figured out that Matriel was killing his gifts to share the meat among the less fortunate, and using the golden skin to raise money for the poor.

It was enraged at being deceived. In that moment, Beta cast a strong curse on Matriel.

“If you really care about all lives, you are going to hold all their sins, now you are the pastor of all souls.”.

After that, every trauma or bad memory was absorbed by Matriel, each memory drained made a new strand of hair grow.

In a few years, all the people who knew her forgot who Matriel was.

Nobody knows, but she is still walking around us.
