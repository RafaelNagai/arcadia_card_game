# Dungeon and Dragon

A long time ago, five individuals lived in peace: a Human, an Elf, a Dwarf, an Avaro and an Orc.

On a magic island, there was a strong spell: only 5 creatures can live and this land would provide everything needed for a long and comfortable life.

Although everyone was happy, one creature was alone in a dungeon… a dragon, living solitary in the cave.

The elder dragon longed to live among them, however since he lived in the deepest dungeon, he knew the place could only support five races, but if one of them swapped places with him, he could live on the surface.

He possessed a crown, a lost treasure from the dungeon, which he offered to the Human who quickly accepted it. Although it was a gleaming artifact, nobody knew it was a cursed object.

The moment the simple human wore the crown, he was possessed by something evil.

The human killed the Orc.

The human tamed the Avaro.

The human let the Dwarf escape.

the human fought against the Elf.

The Dwarf escaped to the dungeon and spent his entire life digging new tunnels in the cave.

The elf coveted the crown, and they are still fighting to this day.

And the dragon? he didn’t need to leave… he had a friend inside the dungeon now.
